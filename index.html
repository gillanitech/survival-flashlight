Frequently Asked Questions

What is Survival Flashlight?

An Android utility with an LED torch, SOS and strobe modes, a front light, battery diagnostics, weather information, and a few extra tools.

Does Survival Flashlight require an account?

No. You can use the app without creating an account.

Why does the app need access to flashlight/camera hardware?

Android exposes the LED torch through the camera hardware, so the app uses that access to switch the light on and off.

Does Survival Flashlight capture photos or videos?

No. The flashlight feature does not require capturing or uploading photos or videos.

What is SOS mode?

A flashing SOS-style light pattern that works as a signaling utility. It is not a guaranteed emergency or rescue feature.

What does Strobe mode do?

It flashes the light rapidly on and off.

What battery information can I view?

Battery percentage, temperature, voltage, and health/status. This is processed locally on your device.

Does the app show weather information?

Yes, weather information is available inside the app.

Does Survival Flashlight contain ads?

The current version contains no ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
